# 🚀 HR Onboarding Assistant - Hackathon Pitch

## The Problem

**HR teams spend 40% of their time on repetitive administrative tasks** instead of focusing on what matters: people.

Every new employee onboarding involves:
- ✉️ Sending welcome emails
- 📅 Scheduling calendar events
- 📄 Creating documents
- 💬 Posting announcements
- ✅ Creating task lists
- 🔄 Coordinating across 5+ different tools

**This is manual, error-prone, and time-consuming.**

## Our Solution

**HR Onboarding Assistant** - An AI-powered agent built with **IBM watsonx Orchestrate** that automates the entire onboarding workflow across all your tools.

### What It Does

One click → Complete onboarding orchestration:

1. **Sends personalized welcome emails** via Gmail
2. **Creates calendar events** for orientation sessions
3. **Generates onboarding documents** in Google Docs
4. **Posts announcements** in Slack channels
5. **Creates task lists** in Jira
6. **Sends confirmation** to HR team

All orchestrated seamlessly by IBM watsonx Orchestrate.
